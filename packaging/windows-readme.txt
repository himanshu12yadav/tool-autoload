Auto Reload for Windows
=======================

1. Install Google Chrome (or Microsoft Edge) if you do not have it. The app drives that browser.
2. Double-click AutoReload.exe.
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not code-signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Type a URL, choose how often to reload (Every), Normal or Hard reload, then click
   "Add job" and "Start".

Other options on the form
  Reloads   stop after this many reloads (or tick Infinite)
  Tabs      open the URL in several tabs and reload them together
  Run for   stop by itself after days / hours / minutes / seconds (all 0 = no limit)

Signing in with Google
  Google refuses automated browsers. Tick "Use my own Chrome", click "Open Chrome",
  sign in in that window, then click "Start".

Your jobs and logins are kept in %APPDATA%\AutoReload.
